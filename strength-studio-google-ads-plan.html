Section 00

Strategic overview

$1,400/month across two Google Ads campaigns — one for brand-adjacent traffic to strength.sg, one for lead-gen conversions on strength.sg/start. Delivered as Search RSAs with a small remarketing layer on the traffic campaign to maximise budget efficiency.

Budget efficiency flag A pure "cold traffic to homepage" campaign is the least efficient Google Ads spend for a new local business at this budget level. [Certain] Homepage traffic without a defined conversion action produces no measurable outcome, and generic fitness terms in Singapore cost $1.50–3 per click. This plan runs the "traffic" campaign as brand search + retargeting instead — same $420 budget, meaningfully better efficiency. If you'd rather run pure cold awareness, the plan can be rebuilt but expect ~50% weaker cost-per-visit efficiency.

Why this split (70/30 weighted to lead gen)

[Certain] Lead gen with high-intent search terms and a clear conversion action produces measurable ROI. Every dollar has an attributable outcome (form submission). Traffic campaigns provide brand-support value that's real but harder to measure.

[Likely] For a new studio with ~4 weeks of Meta campaigns already running, Google Ads works best as the "capture" layer — catching people who saw Meta ads, searched later, and are now in high-intent decision mode. That's what the Search RSA campaign structure is designed for.

Once you have 30+ days of conversion data, consider shifting to 80/20 in favour of lead gen if the lead campaign is producing under $25 CPA.

Section 01

Setup requirements (before launch)

Non-negotiable pre-launch setup. Skipping any of these produces campaigns that spend budget without generating measurable conversion signal.

1. Conversion tracking

[Certain] Verified: GA4 (G-3R5HK6K6DP) and GTM (GTM-MGF33GP6) are already live on strength.sg. This is the foundation.

What still needs setup:

▸ Create a form_submit event in GA4 that fires when the /start page form is successfully submitted (GTM trigger: form submission on /start)

▸ Mark form_submit as a "Key Event" in GA4 admin (was called "Conversion" previously)

▸ Link GA4 to Google Ads account (Admin → Google Ads Links)

▸ In Google Ads, import the form_submit key event as a conversion action

▸ Set the conversion value to $150 (regular price of assessment — this becomes the notional "revenue" per conversion for Google's Smart Bidding)

2. Secondary conversion actions (for full-funnel visibility)

Even if the primary optimisation goal is form submission, tracking these helps Google's algorithm and gives you fuller diagnostic data:

▸ WhatsApp click (any tap on the wa.me link) → GA4 event: whatsapp_click

▸ Phone number click → GA4 event: phone_click

▸ Click through to book.strength.sg → GA4 event: booking_link_click

These get imported as secondary conversions in Google Ads (not the bidding target, but visible in reports).

3. Google Business Profile

Ensure Google Business Profile is set up and verified for 77 Upper East Coast Road. This enables:

▸ Location extension on Google Ads (address + directions link in the ad)

▸ Google Maps listing appearing for "personal trainer near me" searches

▸ Reviews accumulation (which feed into ad quality signals)

If not set up yet, do this before launching ads — it takes 1-2 weeks for Google to verify a new business.

4. Remarketing audience

Create an audience in GA4: "All strength.sg visitors, past 30 days, did NOT submit form_submit event." This becomes the retargeting audience for Campaign 1's Display component.

Timeline reality check All four setup items above take 1-2 weeks combined if starting from scratch. Google Business Profile verification alone can take 5-7 business days. Plan launch for 2 weeks out from setup start, not tomorrow.

Section 04

Launch checklist

Everything to tick off before the first ad goes live. Do these in order.

Pre-launch (2 weeks out)

  • Google Business Profile verified and live at 77 Upper East Coast Road. Add photos of studio interior, exterior, and one team photo.
  • GA4 form_submit event created via GTM trigger on /start page form submission. Test using GA4 DebugView before publishing.
  • Mark form_submit as Key Event in GA4 admin.
  • Link GA4 to Google Ads account.
  • Import form_submit as conversion action in Google Ads with $150 conversion value.
  • Set up secondary conversion events: whatsapp_click, phone_click, booking_link_click.
  • Create GA4 remarketing audience: strength.sg visitors past 30 days, excluding form submitters.
  • Import audience to Google Ads for Campaign 1's Display component.

Campaign build (week of launch)

  • Campaign 1: Create Search + Display campaign, structure with 2 ad groups (Brand Search + Display Remarketing), enter keywords and ads from Section 02.
  • Campaign 2: Create Search campaign, structure with 3 ad groups (Trial & Assessment, Cheap & Deal, Local + Training), enter keywords and ads from Section 03.
  • Assets library: Add all sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, location extension, call extension, promotion extension.
  • Location bid adjustments: +20% for Siglap, Marine Parade, Bedok, Katong, Kembangan, Tanjong Katong.
  • Budget caps: $14/day Campaign 1, $32/day Campaign 2. Enable shared budget only if you plan to shift between campaigns dynamically.
  • Ad Preview and Diagnosis: Run a test search for each keyword to confirm ads render correctly before enabling.
  • Enable enhanced conversions for lead form submission (uses hashed email/phone from form for improved conversion matching).

First 7 days of live campaigns

  • Daily budget monitoring: check daily to catch runaway spend or bid issues.
  • Search terms report: check every 2 days. Add poor-fit terms to negative keyword list.
  • Ad Strength score: aim for "Good" or "Excellent" per ad. If "Poor", add more headlines/descriptions.
  • Do not change bids or budgets in the first 7 days. Google's algorithm needs a stable learning period.
  • Track lead quality: how many of the form submissions are showing up to their Training Assessment? Cost per booking > cost per form submission.

Day 30 review

  • Bidding strategy switch: if Campaign 2 has 15+ conversions, switch from Maximize Conversions to Target CPA at $20 SGD.
  • Budget rebalance: if Campaign 2's CPA is under $25, consider shifting $100–200 from Campaign 1 into Campaign 2.
  • Pause underperformers: any ad group with 0 conversions after 30 days and $100+ spent should be paused or restructured.
  • Iterate creative: replace the bottom 30% of headlines in the top-performing ads with new variations testing new angles.
  • Add negative keywords: search terms report should have 20-40 negatives added by end of month 1.

Campaign 2 · Lead Gen

Form submissions: 35–60 in month 1 [Likely]

Cost per lead: $16–28 SGD [Likely]

Click-through rate: 4–8% (Search RSAs on qualified keywords) [Certain range]

Conversion rate: 3–7% of clicks convert to form submission [Likely — depends heavily on landing page performance]
