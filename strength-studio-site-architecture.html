Section 04

Integration & handoff points

How the Webflow site connects to Rezerv, social channels, and third-party services.

Rezerv — book.strength.sg
Every "Book" CTA across the site links to book.strength.sg. The Rezerv platform handles:
  • Session booking (Movement Assessment, Solo/Duo/Crew, Physio)
  • Package purchases (Foundation / Momentum / Mastery)
  • Membership management and payments
  • Client accounts and scheduling

Where possible, "Book" CTAs should deep-link to the specific service on Rezerv (e.g. book.strength.sg/movement-assessment) rather than the generic timetable. This reduces friction and improves conversion.

Section 05

Implementation notes

Pricing visibility. The Membership page includes a pricing tiers section, but consider keeping pricing as "Enquire" rather than public at launch. Premium studios often do this to qualify leads and avoid direct price comparison with standard gyms.
Founding Member toggle. The Membership page has a "Founding Member callout" block designed to be switched on during pre-launch and hidden post-launch via Webflow's conditional visibility. No separate landing page needed.
Blog → Trainer linking. Blog posts reference the Trainers CMS collection as author. This means every article automatically links back to the trainer's profile page — strengthening internal linking, SEO, and team authority positioning.
Offers with date logic. The Offers collection includes start/end dates and an active toggle. Use Webflow's conditional visibility to auto-hide expired offers from /happenings while keeping detail pages alive for SEO value.
No GoFit reference. Strength Studio operates as a completely separate brand. The only parent company reference is "JSF Transformative Pte Ltd" in the footer copyright line.
