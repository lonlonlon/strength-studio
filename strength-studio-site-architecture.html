Section 04

Integration & handoff points

How the Webflow site connects to Rezerv, social channels, and third-party services.

Rezerv — book.strength.sg
Every "Book" CTA across the site links to book.strength.sg. The Rezerv platform handles:
  • Session booking (Movement Assessment, Solo/Duo, Recovery)
  • Training package purchases (Foundation / Momentum / Progression / Mastery) — primary revenue path
  • Membership management and payments — secondary path for returning clients
  • Client accounts and scheduling

Where possible, "Book" CTAs should deep-link to the specific service on Rezerv (e.g. book.strength.sg/movement-assessment) rather than the generic timetable. This reduces friction and improves conversion.

Section 05

Implementation notes

Training-first IA. Every new client begins with a training package. The /join page and Join Us nav lead with Training Packages as the primary path; Membership is positioned as a secondary option for clients who have completed a package. This shapes hero copy, visual weight on the /join landing page, and the CTA hierarchy throughout the site.
Pricing not public. Training package pricing is intentionally kept off the website. Tier names, session counts, and "best for" positioning are shown, but dollar figures are reserved for internal reference and post-trial conversations. The Training Packages page CTAs route to "Enquire" or "Book a trial." This qualifies leads and avoids direct price comparison with standard gyms. Membership ($200/month, 12-month) can be shown since it's gated by eligibility rather than competing for new visitors.
Founding Client toggle. The Training Packages page has a "Founding Client callout" block designed to be switched on during pre-launch and hidden post-launch via Webflow's conditional visibility. No separate landing page needed. The founding cohort is positioned around the first training-package clients, not membership.
Blog → Coach linking. Blog posts reference the Coaches CMS collection as author. This means every article automatically links back to the coach's profile page — strengthening internal linking, SEO, and team authority positioning.
Offers with date logic. The Offers collection includes start/end dates and an active toggle. Use Webflow's conditional visibility to auto-hide expired offers from /happenings while keeping detail pages alive for SEO value.
No GoFit reference. Strength Studio operates as a completely separate brand. The only parent company reference is "JSF Transformative Pte Ltd" in the footer copyright line.
